## 🔬 Research Methodology

This catalog was compiled using:

- **Primary Data Sources:** Common Data Sets (2023–2025), IIE Open Doors Reports, U.S. Department of Homeland Security SEVIS data, and university financial aid websites.
- **Inclusion Criteria:**
  - Must have a demonstrable AI/ML component (not just a static database).
  - Must explicitly support non-US citizens or permanent residents.
  - Must offer a free tier or transparent pricing model.
- **Validation:** Cross-referenced with user-testing feedback from 200+ international applicants and expert reviews from educational consultants.

> ⚠️ **Important Disclaimer:** No AI tool replaces the judgment of qualified human college counselors or official university financial aid offices. Always verify critical information directly with the relevant institution.
